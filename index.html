Build Your Socratic Questions

Strong seminar questions invite interpretation, evidence, and multiple viewpoints.
Weak/factual: What is text messaging?
Stronger/Socratic: How does losing facial expression and body language change the way we interpret a message?
Useful stems: Why might…?   To what extent…?   How does…?   Which is more effective when…?   What evidence challenges…?   How might communication change if…?
